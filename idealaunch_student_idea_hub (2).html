Demystifying Tech

How ideas become working software

You don't need to write thousands of lines of syntax. Software is simply four intuitive pieces working together. Here is how you can understand every application.

STAGE 01

1. Idea & Problem

Before any code, clearly articulate who is suffering and how your product fixes it. The best apps start as one honest observation on paper.

Deliverable: 1-Page Concept Pitch
STAGE 02

2. Front End

The digital storefront. It's the buttons, text, and graphics people tap on their phone. Modern tools let you construct this visually like LEGO pieces.

Deliverable: Interactive UI Mockup
STAGE 03

3. Database

The collective memory. When someone signs up, posts a card, or upvotes, the database securely preserves that information for the next time they visit.

Deliverable: Clean Spreadsheet/Data Schema
STAGE 04

4. Deployment

Putting your app onto the open internet. With free hosting services today, you can turn your project files into a public .com link with a single click.

Deliverable: Shareable Public Link
